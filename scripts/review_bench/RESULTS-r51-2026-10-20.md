rule or an import side effect excuses a `dup.cross-file` copy only if no
shared home meets it, a new module included. The `dup.cross-file` row changed,
so the battery and the confirm arm were re-run on dev, then once on test.

`RESULTS-r50-2026-10-18.md`. These numbers describe these prompts and labels;
rerun rather than quote.
