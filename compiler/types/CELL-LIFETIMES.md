# Typechecker cell lifetimes

One row per piece of mutable typechecker state: the five module-level cells of `compiler/types/typecheck.mdk`, the fields of its bundle records (`ModuleCtx`, `GraphCtx`, `GraphRun`, `CrossRun`, declared in `compiler/types/tc_types.mdk`), the fields of the process's interners (`Interners`, `Lineage`), the fields of the records that carry the drive's configuration (`TcConfig`, `DriveInputs`, `TraceSink`) and of the context records that replace the old cells (`GroupCtx`, `DynCtx`, `SiteCtx`), and the cells outside the file that a drive reads or writes. It is the inventory the state-by-lifetime split works from.

Counts: 5 module-level cells in `types.typecheck`; field rows `ModuleCtx` 91, `GraphRun` 30, `CrossRun` 14, `GraphCtx` 20, `Interners` 2, `Lineage` 3, `TcConfig` 5, `DriveInputs` 6, `TraceSink` 8; 5 rows outside the file. The first table is pinned to the cell census by `test/arch_boundaries_test.mdk`.

Stage-6 denominators (`python3 -I fnmap.py compiler/types/typecheck.mdk refs|closure|frontier <word> --holders 'TcEnv|DynCtx|SiteCtx|ModuleCtx|GraphCtx'` at the head of arch-p2-6c, 3434 top-level functions): functions that mention the word / functions that reach one / functions that need a new parameter if its state moves (`frontier`, with the carrier holders as stops). `graphCtx` 276 / 1457 / 463; `interners` 4 / 59 / 2; `currentLocRef` (a `ModuleCtx` field since the cell `currentLoc` was deleted) 84 / 1458 / 3. `perRun` is deleted (arch-p2-6c): the module record is a parameter of its readers or comes through `DynCtx.dcModule` and `SiteCtx.sxModule`, and 6d takes the graph record the same way.

Columns: `name | holds | lifetime | owner-today | writers | reset | target | interface-fills | source | issue`.

- `lifetime` is the measured one, from the writers and the reset site, not the declaring comment: `process` (never reset), `config` (set by the driver before the drive), `graph` (re-minted per graph), `module` (re-minted per module), `group` (reset per check group), `run` (saved and restored around one walk), `unknown`.
- `target` is where the cell lands: `TcEnv` (run), `GroupCtx`, `ModuleCtx`, `GraphCtx`, `TcConfig`, `interner` (stays process-wide), `delete` (dead or test-only).
- `interface-fills` is `yes` when a loaded ModuleInterface would have to populate the cell for an importer to typecheck against the module without re-checking it. `source` then names the published fields it derives from (`cpSchemes cpCore cpEvidence cpDispositions`), `derived(<field>)` where a computation over one reproduces it, or `none`. `cpBinders` does not exist in `CheckedProgram` at this base, so binder rows have source `none`.
- Bundle container cell (`graphCtx`) and `GraphCtx`'s sub-record and configuration fields (`GraphCtx.crossRun`, `GraphCtx.graphRun`, `GraphCtx.config`, `GraphCtx.trace`) are rows of their own; their fields are the rows that carry the lifetimes. `graphCtx` is still a shim (sprint 6d threads the GraphCtx and deletes it); the row says what happens to it. `DriveInputs` is not a cell: it is the drive's first argument, and its field rows say who builds it. `DriveOutputs` (`tc_types.mdk`) is not a cell record either: it is the plain data a drive returns, frozen at the drive's end and holding no `Ref`, so it has no rows.
- "Interface fills (input to playbook C1)", after the second table, is outside the parsed tables: it names, for each row the awk `$8=="yes" && $9=="none"` selects (and for the caveat rows), the field that owns it, its producer and its kind.

## Interface fills (input to playbook C1)

The rows of the typechecker tables whose `interface-fills` is `yes` and whose `source` is `none` (the strict rows), plus the rows whose `source` is `derived(cpSchemes)` that the cell table's first review left as a caveat (the function-versus-method split of the predicate slots, and `dictEligible*`). A loaded `ModuleInterface` would have to restore each one for an importer to typecheck against a module without re-checking it. `kind` is what C1 needs: a `computed once` value is rebuilt from the loaded graph's declarations in one call; an `accumulated` value grows as each module is checked, so C1 restores it from each loaded module's interface. The two counters `GraphRun.tyvarCounter` and `GraphRun.effvarCounter` are also `derived(cpSchemes)` but are id sources, not interface content, and are not listed.
