RFC 3174 section 7.3 "Test Driver" — the four SHA-1 test vectors, transcribed
from the test driver's own testarray / repeatcount / resultarray literals.
The first three are the tests documented in FIPS PUB 180-1; the fourth is an
exact multiple of 512 bits.

Each record: the message pattern, how many times it is repeated to form the
message, and the expected digest as the RFC prints it (20 bytes, space
separated hex).

TEST1
  pattern: abc
  repeat:  1
  digest:  A9 99 3E 36 47 06 81 6A BA 3E 25 71 78 50 C2 6C 9C D0 D8 9D

TEST2
  pattern: abcdbcdecdefdefgefghfghighijhijkijkljklmklmnlmnomnopnopq
  repeat:  1
  digest:  84 98 3E 44 1C 3B D2 6E BA AE 4A A1 F9 51 29 E5 E5 46 70 F1

TEST3
  pattern: a
  repeat:  1000000
  digest:  34 AA 97 3C D4 C4 DA A4 F6 1E EB 2B DB AD 27 31 65 34 01 6F

TEST4
  pattern: 0123456701234567012345670123456701234567012345670123456701234567
  repeat:  10
  digest:  DE A3 56 A2 CD DD 90 C7 A7 EC ED C5 EB B5 63 93 4F 46 04 52

TEST4's pattern is the eight characters "01234567" repeated eight times, with
no separator of any kind; the RFC spells it as TEST4a TEST4b, two 32-character
halves.
