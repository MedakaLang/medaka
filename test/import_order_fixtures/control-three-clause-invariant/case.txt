what: CONTROL — a THREE-clause entry, so all 3! orderings are exercised, not just a reversal

The 2-clause cases in this corpus have exactly one non-identity permutation, so they
cannot tell a full-reversal permuter from an all-orderings one. This case has three
import clauses and therefore six orderings; a defect that depended on an ADJACENT-pair
position rather than on first/last would be invisible to a reversal-only differential
(the limitation test/diff_compiler_dict_semantics.sh's Section 4 records for itself).

`mid` re-exports `base`, and `leaf` shadows nothing; every ordering must give the same
answer. Nothing here is expected to be order-sensitive — it is the row that proves the
N>2 permutation machinery actually runs, and it goes red if it stops.
