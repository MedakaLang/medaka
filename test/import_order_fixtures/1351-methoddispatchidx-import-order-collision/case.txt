## HOW THE EXPECTED VALUES WERE ESTABLISHED

⚠️ These are NOT adjudicated correct answers -- no spec ruling exists for what
`mth "abc" 5` should mean when two unrelated interfaces share a bare method name at
DIFFERING dispatch indices plus a standalone shadow. #1351's own body derives (by
hand, from the declared signatures, independently of this gate) that `7` -- the
body of `impl IZ String`, whose `mth` takes an `Int` first -- cannot be reached
correctly by a call whose first argument is a `String`; but it explicitly does NOT
settle whether the "right" answer is the standalone's `99` or a rejected program.
**What this row pins is the DIVERGENCE, not a verdict on which side is correct.**

itself printed for this case while UNLEDGERED, transcribed verbatim (not

3 of the 6 orderings print 7 at exit 0 (whenever `amodI`'s clause precedes
`zmodI`'s); the other 3 reject (whenever `zmodI`'s clause precedes `amodI`'s) --
an exact 3/3 split on which of the two colliding interfaces' import clause comes
first, consistent with the "first-match wins" mechanism #1351 names
(`universeMethodDispatchIdxRef` / `methodDispatchIdx`, both accumulated by
concatenation and read by first match). This matches the pair #1351's own issue
body and its must-fail pin
(`test/must_fail_fixtures/1351-methoddispatchidx-import-order-collision/`) report
for their two named orderings (`main.mdk` = fmodI,amodI,zmodI here; `order-swapped`
= fmodI,zmodI,amodI here). Both `run` and `build` were graded by this gate and agree
with each other within every ordering, consistent with #1351's own "native arm"
measurement that `medaka build` is not an independent observation of this defect
(same shared front end).
