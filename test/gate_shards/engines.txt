`diff_compiler_engines` is the single most expensive gate in the
suite and irreducible — every fixture pays a full `medaka build` +
clang, on THREE engines. `full_cores` gives it the whole box rather than the
gate's own ENGINE_JOBS=NCPU/2 hedge (right when sharing a runner,
wasteful when not — it doesn't, here). `wasm_arm` (#597) installs
wasm-tools + Node and builds the Wasm oracle so this gate's T2/T3
tiers actually run; MEDAKA_REQUIRE_WASM=1 makes a degraded run a hard
fail so the wiring cannot silently rot back. diff_compiler_rejection_parity
(#709) and Phase 2's focused PDS protocol gate ride along here,
needing the same toolchain. The protocol gate was placed after
re-deriving the available `wasm_arm` rows with the cost tool; its
local timing is recorded in the sprint report.
